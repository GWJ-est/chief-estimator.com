# Chief-Estimator

Chief-Estimator helps residential and light commercial contractors build interior estimates in Claude. It was written by a licensed Gulf Coast contractor with 40 years of field and engineering experience.

## What it does

The Residential Interior Estimate skill takes a contractor from room dimensions to a finished bid summary. It works for bathroom remodels, kitchen updates, bedrooms, living areas, and whole-house interior refreshes.

- Asks for the job facts in one short batch: rooms, dimensions, scope, finish level, labor rates, overhead, profit, and how sales tax applies.
- Calculates quantities room by room and shows the math, including floor, wall, and ceiling areas, drywall sheets, paint gallons, trim, flooring, and tile with waste.
- Prices labor and materials in separate columns.
- Checks for commonly missed items such as permits, dumpster and haul-off, protection, consumables, supervision, hidden-condition contingency, lead-safe work on pre-1978 homes, and final cleaning.
- Applies overhead, profit, and sales tax in order and warns when a markup will not produce the margin the contractor expects.
- Reviews an existing bid and lists problems with the largest dollar impact first.

The skill never makes up local prices. If a rate is missing, it asks for it or marks the line "Rate needed."

## How to use it

Add the plugin, then describe the job in plain words. Example prompts:

1. "Price a 5x8 hall bath gut and rebuild, mid-grade finishes, New Orleans."
2. "Estimate paint and LVP flooring for three bedrooms: 12x12, 11x10, and 10x10, 8 ft ceilings."
3. "Check my kitchen bid for missing items before I send it."

## What it runs and sends

This plugin contains instructions only. It runs no scripts, connects to no outside services, and collects, stores, or sends no data. Everything stays inside your Claude conversation.

## Troubleshooting

- If Claude does not use the skill, mention that you want an estimate, a bid, or a price for an interior job.
- If a quantity looks wrong, check the dimensions and door and window counts you gave. The skill shows every formula so you can spot the input that is off.
- Waste factors and default door and window sizes are starting points. Tell Claude your numbers and it will use them.
